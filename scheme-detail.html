Scheme Summary & Investment Objective

The investment objective of SBI Arbitrage Opportunities Fund is to provide capital appreciation and regular income for investors through arbitrage opportunities in the cash and derivatives segments of the equity market alongside investments in debt securities and money market instruments.

Who Should Invest?

  • Investors seeking low risk returns similar to short term debt funds.
  • Ideal for parking surplus funds for a 3 to 12 months horizon.
  • Tax advantage of Equity funds with debt-like stability.

Taxation Guide (Equity Oriented)

  • STCG: 20% if redeemed before 12 months.
  • LTCG: 12.5% for gains above ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year.
  • Treated as Equity fund because equity holding > 65%.
